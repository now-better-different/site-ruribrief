FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you use AI in your research?

Yes, openly. AI tooling lets us read and structure far more Japanese-language material than a human researcher could in the same time. Every deliverable is source-verified and reviewed by a human before delivery, and we never ship unedited AI output. If your platform or policy requires AI disclosure, we comply fully.

Really no meetings?

Really. Scoping happens in writing — it's faster and leaves a record. If you strongly prefer a call, we offer a paid 25-minute written-summary call on Standard-tier projects and above.

What if the research can't answer my question?

If we determine mid-project that public sources can't support a reliable answer, we tell you what we found, what's missing, and refund the difference for undelivered scope. We'd rather lose a fee than ship guesswork.

How do revisions work?

Two rounds of written clarification or revision are included. Additional rounds or scope changes are quoted separately before any work begins.

Who is behind Ruri Brief?

Ruri Brief is operated by a Tokyo-based research LLC. Full legal details are provided in every proposal, contract and invoice. "Ruri" (瑠璃) is the Japanese word for lapis lazuli — the deep blue of careful work.

Can you invoice in JPY? Do you support Japanese clients?

Yes — we invoice in USD or JPY, and deliver in English, Japanese, or both. Japanese clients: see our Japanese page.
