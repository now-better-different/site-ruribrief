FAQ

Questions, answered

Do you use AI in your research?

Yes, openly. AI tooling lets us read and structure far more Japanese-language material than a human researcher could in the same time. Every deliverable is source-verified and reviewed by a human before delivery, and we never ship unedited AI output. If your platform or policy requires AI disclosure, we comply fully.

Is my question confidential?

Yes. Your brief, our findings, and the fact that you asked are never resold, reused or referenced. We do not input your confidential materials into third-party AI tools without your explicit approval. A mutual NDA is available on request at no extra charge on Standard briefs and above.

Really no meetings?

Really. Scoping happens in writing — it's faster and leaves a record. If you'd like to talk, a paid 25-minute call ($60) — with a written summary sent afterward, so the record stays async — is available on Standard briefs and above.

How do I pay?

By card (Stripe, USD) once you approve the quote, or by invoice with bank transfer in USD or JPY for teams that need one. Japanese qualified invoices (適格請求書) available.

When does the clock start?

When scope is confirmed in writing and payment is received. Turnaround is quoted in business days. If we're going to miss an agreed deadline, we tell you immediately and agree a remedy on the affected scope.

What if the research can't answer my question?

If public sources can't support a reliable answer, we deliver a "no reliable public answer" memo — what we searched, what we found, what remains unknown — and refund the undelivered portion of the agreed scope. We'd rather lose a fee than ship guesswork.

How do revisions work?

Two rounds of written clarification or revision are included. Additional rounds or scope changes are quoted separately before any work begins.

Who is behind Ruri Brief?

Ruri Brief is operated by a Tokyo-based research LLC. Corporate details are published on our Legal & Company page and stated in every proposal, contract and invoice. "Ruri" (瑠璃) is the Japanese word for lapis lazuli — the deep blue of careful work.

Can you invoice in JPY? Do you support Japanese clients?

Yes — we invoice in USD or JPY, and deliver in English, Japanese, or both. Japanese clients: see our Japanese page.
